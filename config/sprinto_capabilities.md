# Approved Sprinto capability areas

Pains and emails may ONLY reference these four areas. No pricing, guarantees, time savings or
certification outcomes may be claimed. Sources below were fetched from sprinto.com with the project scraper
(`python -m outreach.capabilities --refresh`).

## compliance preparation

Guided readiness for SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR and other frameworks: scoping, policies, gap tracking and audit preparation.

- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/audits/overview — "This module supports both partner audits, which are linked to specific frameworks (such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, or GDPR), and custom audits, which can be tailored to suit internal policies or third-party requests."
- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/audits/glossary — "SOC 2, ISO 27001), typically available to Plan 1 and 2 users."

## evidence collection

Automated collection of audit evidence from connected cloud, HR and engineering systems instead of manual screenshots and spreadsheets.

- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/audits/overview — "- Real-time visibility into audit progress and evidence collection."
- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/audits/glossary — "An action assigned to a team member to collect or submit evidence for a requirement."

## continuous control monitoring

Ongoing automated checks that controls stay in place between audits, with alerts when something drifts.

- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/audits/overview — "- Real-time visibility into audit progress and evidence collection."
- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/monitors/glossary — "Understand common compliance terms and Sprinto-specific concepts used in the Monitors section with this complete glossary."

## customer trust documentation

Trust center and security documentation that helps answer customer security reviews and questionnaires.

- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/policies/overview — "They are essential for building trust with auditors, customers, and employees."
- source: https://docs.sprinto.com/trust/trust-center — "Sprinto’s Trust Center acts as your unified compliance gateway — designed to help you showcase, organise, and manage your security and compliance posture in a structured and secure manner."
